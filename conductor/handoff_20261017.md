## Decisions taken without being asked (reverse any of them)
- Closing the window during a cleaning stops it after the item it is on (as before); with the menu bar item on, the app stays alive.
- The Monday check uses the window's own scan so a click on the note opens on the same numbers; it never takes the cleaning,
  done, error or confirmation screens from someone using them (it waits and retries every 10 minutes that day).
- A launch caused by "Open at login" opens no window when the menu bar item is on.
- The History face is remembered like List and Map. A pane left open on the List or the map does not turn into the History over a
  scan or an error, and a cleaning that starts closes the pane.
- A run's groups line names only what was deleted and gave the disk space back (failed groups are not named; the Docker chip
  carries Docker), which differs from the board's example.
- The popover's `...` menu shows the board's two key equivalents, so the tripwire on key equivalents allows exactly those two.
